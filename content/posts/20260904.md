* [constrained but not powerless from metafilter](https://www.metafilter.com/214396/constrained-but-not-powerless)

we all know that humam makes mistakes all the time. for us to make less mistakes, one approach is to add frictions. for example, banning smoke indoors. for example, making road narrower slows down the traffic/driving. below is another example to design a badge so that the attachment of it to the product won't go wrong.

> A specific worker was assigned to attach the badge onto the product. It was symmetrical, and was attached with two **same-size** screws. One per minute, 400 per day, 2,000 per week. At a 0.1% error rate, we would find about 2 badges installed upside down per week.

> The solution wasn't to fire them and hire a more conscientious worker, someone who used their "free will" to be more precise in their work. **The solution was to redesign the badge so it took one larger diameter screw and one smaller diameter screw, but keeping the screw head the same size. This made the badge impossible to install incorrectly.**
