> A: A specific worker was assigned to attach the badge onto the product. It was **symmetrical**, and was attached with two (**same-size**) screws. One per minute, 400 per day, 2,000 per week. At a 0.1% error rate, we would find about 2 badges installed upside down per week.

> A: The solution wasn't to fire them and hire a more conscientious worker, someone who used their "free will" to be more precise in their work. **The solution was to redesign the badge so it took one larger diameter screw and one smaller diameter screw, but keeping the screw head the same size. This made the badge impossible to install incorrectly.**
