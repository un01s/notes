> f: The modern Western Buddhist phenomenological observation on free will is that "free will" is debatable, but "free won't" is definitely a human capability. There is also a Western philosophical take on the topic, observing that when it's possible to hold mutually incompatible concepts about reality it's not the Universe's responsibility to be describable by our languages, and the apparent paradox is an artifact of how reality is described, not a quality of reality itself.
